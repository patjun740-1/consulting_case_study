# Consulting Case Studies

Client case projects from my M.S. in Applied Data Science at the University of Chicago (Autumn 2026). In each case, our team acted as a consulting firm: we framed the client's business problem, used data to test it, and presented recommendations to the client and a steering committee of stakeholders.

| Case | Client question | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| [**Verizon: Device-Financing Risk Strategy**](verizon/) | How can Verizon approve, negotiate or deny phone financing faster, more consistently and more profitably in store? | A four-decision in-store tool and pilot plan worth about **+$10.6M per 1M applications** (+$3.2M in the conservative case) |
| [**McDonald's: All-Day Breakfast**](mcdonalds/) | Should McDonald's discontinue, maintain or expand All-Day Breakfast to reverse a ~5% yearly traffic decline? | Showed the 48.6% revenue lift came mostly from bigger orders, not more customers, and set a workplan for the January 1 leadership decision |

## How I approach a case

1. **Frame the decision:** restate the client's question as the decision they actually have to make.
2. **Structure the problem:** break it into testable questions and find where value is won or lost.
3. **Test with data:** check data quality first, then let the evidence confirm or reject each hypothesis.
4. **Recommend and size it:** give a clear answer, a business case with scenarios, and a low-risk way to start.
